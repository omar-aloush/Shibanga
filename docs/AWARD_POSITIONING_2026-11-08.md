# Fringe — award positioning after the Observation Desk

This is a development assessment dated 8 November 2026, not an official judging score or assurance of selection.
The implementation follows one coherent path: **discover → explore → compare → question → share the evidence**.
It is stronger to demonstrate that path clearly than to bolt on unrelated features for every award name.

## Defensible directions

| Direction | What the finished web app demonstrates | Evidence still needed |
|---|---|---|
| Art & Technology | Original illustrated Journey, cinematic field mission, Astrounit guide, dated radar comparison, real-map exploration and actual-data transition | Real audience reaction, final presentation and contribution disclosure |
| Best Use of Storytelling | A lost signal becomes a paired observation, then a field check; the story leads into actual product dates and an investigation brief | Observe whether first-time users understand and complete that transition |
| Best Use of Data | Real provisional archive, product IDs, date-aware comparison, complete residuals/baseline, provenance, reproducible exports and file audit | A stronger independently validated scientific case would materially strengthen this direction |
| Best Use of Technology | Buildless browser app, procedural rendering/audio, keyboard/touch routes, safe local notebook and portable reports | Demonstrate a distinctive technical benefit and measured performance on real devices |
| Best Use of Science | Mechanistic models, transparent methods, synthetic pipeline checks and an exposed unresolved independent comparison | Current GNSS result does not establish strong small-motion accuracy; quantitative claims need improved validation |
| Local Impact | A relevant Delta setting and an observer kit for obtaining real evidence | Actual target users, permission-based sessions, documented needs and outcomes; no adoption is claimed |

Global Connection, Galactic Impact, Best Mission Concept and Most Inspirational should not be treated as automatic
fits. Multiple sites do not establish global collaboration, and an attractive game does not establish adoption.
The project may be considered along more than one award direction; this does not establish eligibility for multiple wins.

## Lessons from confirmed previous winners

NASA's 2025 winner announcement describes **Zumorroda-X** (Art & Technology) as agricultural mini-games,
**HerCode Space** (Storytelling) as an illustrated heliophysics story, and **Resonant Exoplanets** (Data) as an
automated analysis system. The useful lesson is an integrated, demonstrable experience and a clear contribution.
These descriptions are not a basis for a numerical probability of Fringe winning.

## A short demonstration

1. **0:00–0:25** — The human problem: people see radar colours but need dates, context and a way to check an interpretation.
2. **0:25–1:05** — Show actual play: movement, scanning, A/B pairing and the fictional field check. Label any edited time compression.
3. **1:05–1:35** — Astrounit guides a recorded-date comparison. Move into Inspect, swipe and pin a visible pattern.
4. **1:35–2:05** — Save a visual observation separately from its hypothesis and next check. Download the portable brief.
5. **2:05–2:35** — Show acquisition IDs, historical GNSS limits and the full unresolved station comparison. Explain what further evidence is needed.
6. **2:35–3:00** — State the intended audience, what works today, what was actually tested and the next real-user/validation step.

Use actual product dates. Do not pass a seeded QA checkpoint off as live mission completion, or a synthetic observer
session off as participant evidence. Do not claim flood classification, centimetre precision, official endorsement or users not observed.
