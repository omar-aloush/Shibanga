This optional observer tool helps you run a real usability session. It contains no pre-filled participant results. Records stay in this browser until you export them; nothing is uploaded.

Before the session

Explain the activity, ask permission, and let the participant stop at any time. Record no names, contact details, precise locations or sensitive information. Use a random participant code.

Starting a session creates an empty local record. Timings are active task time; pauses and hidden pages stop the timer. They are usability observations, not proof of learning or scientific validity.

Watch first. Help only when needed.

Read each task once. Start its timer, observe what happens, then record the outcome and the assistance provided. Explanations should be in the participant’s own words.
